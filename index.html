GTA6 Cuts

A personal tool for a single creator.

GTA6 Cuts is a private desktop tool that prepares short vertical gaming clips on the creator's own computer and sends them to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts (via the TikTok Content Posting API, "Upload" flow).

Nothing is published automatically. Every video arrives in the creator's TikTok inbox, where the creator reviews it, adds music and a caption, and decides whether to post it.

How it works

  1. The creator signs in with TikTok once and grants the video.upload permission.
  2. The tool uploads a prepared clip to the creator's TikTok inbox.
  3. The creator opens the notification in the TikTok app, edits the video and posts it manually.

The tool is used only by its owner and is not offered to other users.
