Privacy Policy

GTA6 Cuts · Last updated: November 14, 2026

GTA6 Cuts ("the app") is a personal tool used by its owner to upload video drafts to the owner's own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we access

  • TikTok account identifier (open_id) and access tokens issued by TikTok after you sign in and grant the video.upload permission.
  • Video files that the owner chooses to upload, together with their metadata (file size, upload status).

How data is used

The data is used only to upload videos to the TikTok inbox of the account that authorized the app and to check the status of those uploads. The app does not read your followers, messages, analytics or any other account data.

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. The app has no server and does not send your data to any third party other than TikTok's official API.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is in use. You can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions. Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless; the owner deletes them from the local computer.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and is not used by them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
